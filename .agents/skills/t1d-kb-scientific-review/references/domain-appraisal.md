# Domain appraisal

Select the applicable domains; a whole-base audit includes each.

## Games, studies and outcomes

Separate the product, the evaluated intervention/version, and individual study reports. Extract intended and enrolled populations, comparator, exposure, delivery/support, recruitment, attrition, analysis population, endpoints, follow-up and effect estimates. Check whether the outcome supports the catalogue's evidence summary. A protocol establishes planned methods, not benefit. A controlled design may still have serious bias.

Appraise allocation and comparability, outcome measurement, missing data, selective reporting, contamination and co-interventions as appropriate to design. For qualitative or co-design studies, assess sampling, context, participant roles, analysis and reflexivity rather than treating lack of randomisation as failure. Select established appraisal/reporting instruments appropriate to the design, verifying their current instructions before formal use; do not claim an instrument was applied merely because its name is cited. A whole intervention's effect does not identify the effect of each mechanic.

## Learning, psychology and human factors

Trace the argument from learning objective to activity, hypothesised mechanism, measured construct, result and transfer boundary. Separate theory, experimental evidence, association and design inference. Check learner age, prior knowledge, newly diagnosed status, caregiver role, setting, exposure, instrument validity and delayed assessment. Evaluate psychosocial outcomes on their own terms rather than requiring glycaemic endpoints. Inspect accessibility and onboarding advice for evidence or clear normative rationale. Do not present industry conventions as measured educational effects.

## Physiology and modelling

Check human/T1D specificity, measurement conditions, units, uncertainty and mechanisms against sources. Distinguish endogenous physiology from exogenous treatment kinetics, mathematical state from literal anatomy, model fit from predictive validity, and physiological validity from learning validity. Quantitative claims require source support even in compressed overview chapters. Model families may be compared generically; do not add implementation material for the affiliated simulator. Explain which simplifications are acceptable for the stated learning objective and which risk teaching a false causal relation.

## Availability and historical products

Check developer/owner provenance, public versus study-only release, platform, region, price, account and hardware requirements, dates and versions. Record what was observed: page reachable, listing exists, download offered, installation tested, or playtested. Do not upgrade one observation to another. No current link does not prove discontinuation. Preserve uncertain migration, obsolete hardware, archived access and historical availability distinctions. Store documented lived-experience involvement rather than assuming it from project origin.

## Adoption, sustainability and reusable design lessons

Differentiate unavailable-at-audit from disappeared-after-release. Test funding, staff departure, multidisciplinary maintenance and brochure comparisons as hypotheses unless project histories or other evidence establish causation. Transfer software/health-service findings with their original population and setting visible. Open source and AI may support maintenance; neither establishes sustained adoption or clinical benefit. Explain concrete proposed workflows and the evidence needed to test them.

## Cross-domain synthesis

Check consistency across overview, chapter, game profile, table, glossary and explorer. A general evidence label must not conceal mixed populations or unmatched outcomes. Represent contrary findings and uncertainty proportionately. Give each game the same appraisal criteria and disclose project affiliations without granting evidentiary priority. Identify missing topics through a documented search, not personal expectations alone.
