# Full-Text Access Wishlist

**Scope:** Serious games, gamification, simulations, and related physiological, educational and implementation-science sources used in this knowledge base, with priority given to type 1 diabetes (T1D).

**Last checked:** 6 September 2026; individual attempts are recorded in the audit access register.

**Purpose:** Sources for which a stable, lawfully accessible full text could not be verified. These records may be revisited through institutional library access, interlibrary loan, or direct author contact.

This list includes subscription barriers, unsuccessful open-access retrievals and unresolved publication identities; these are not interchangeable. Lawful private access and permission to redistribute a publication are separate questions. Missing permission to republish is not, by itself, a reason to reject a lawfully accessible reading copy.

The [6 September acquisition backlog](../docs/reviews/2026-09-06_scientific-content-audit/full-text-backlog.md) extends this priority list to all unresolved linked scholarly sources encountered in the scientific audit. Its [source-access register](../docs/reviews/2026-09-06_scientific-content-audit/source-access-register.json) records attempted routes and separates retained candidate files from scientific verification. The priority records below are retained for continuity; they do not constitute the complete backlog.

## 5. Suggested access routes

1. Use an authorised institutional subscription or interlibrary-loan service.
2. Request a lawful author manuscript from the corresponding author.
3. Recheck trusted repositories for later open-access versions, corrections, or supplementary material.
