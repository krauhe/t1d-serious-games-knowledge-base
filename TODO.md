## Future design work

1. **Scientific-first visual redesign**

   **Added:** 2026-09-05

   **Status:** Future design exploration

   Develop a primarily scientific and editorial website layout in which evidence, citations, tables, comparison tools and long-form reading remain visually dominant. Introduce a restrained original arcade-inspired layer through background illustrations, section markers, pixel-scale details or occasional game-interface references. The arcade treatment must remain subordinate to readability and scientific credibility, must not reproduce protected visual elements from existing games, and must preserve responsive behaviour, accessibility, reduced-motion support and print/PDF legibility.
