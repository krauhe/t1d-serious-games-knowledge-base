## Scope and completed changes

1. **Learning science — FIXED, 2026-09-16.** Expanded comparative evidence, explanatory feedback, scaffolding and retrieval/transfer. Added study populations, comparison structure, effect estimates and boundaries of inference. Proposed simulation loops remain labelled as design inferences, not tested T1D interventions.
2. **Game mechanisms — FIXED, 2026-09-16.** Added gamification effect estimates and the less conclusive rigorous-design subsets, the inability to attribute multicomponent effects to points, a cancer-game trial with outcome denominators, and mixed diabetes-specific self-determination findings. Ethical proposals are separated from empirical effects.
3. **Evaluation — FIXED, 2026-09-16.** Added detailed interpretation of three T1D studies, including randomisation of test forms rather than exposure, intention to continue versus measured use, and procedural distress versus broader psychological outcomes. Added independent reasoning assessment, comparator choice, missingness and process evaluation without repeating the game catalogue.
4. **Human factors — FIXED, 2026-09-16.** Added tutorial field-experiment results, default/choice findings, psychosocial objectives and specific accessibility requirements. Distinguishes unnecessary interface operations from productive instructional effort, and clinical communication guidance from tested game interventions.

Source chapter word counts, including tables and references, changed from 897 to 2,233; 896 to 2,295; 993 to 2,244; and 749 to 2,176, respectively. These counts document increased depth, not a quality target. No game catalogue was duplicated. New prose was synthesised from checked evidence; historical wording was not blindly restored.
